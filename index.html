In daily use

Isaano · root-cause engine

Named after the Kinyarwanda isano, the link. It reads alarms, counters, traces and the vendor manual for the exact release, then writes the root-cause report, the evidence pack and the change procedure with rollback. When evidence is missing it asks for exactly what is needed instead of guessing.

4 h → 15 min diagnosis and RCA time in production operations, with automation I built.

Version 2 · 2026

Manual-to-evidence pipeline

Turns a vendor documentation library into a verified dictionary that the engine is allowed to quote. Every counter and alarm passes the vendor's exact ID grammar before use; full-text plus semantic search; nothing answered from memory.

72,430 verified counters and 1,255 alarm codes, up from 18,131 counters in version 1 (2023–2025).
