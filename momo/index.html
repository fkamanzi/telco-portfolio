Where Did My MoMo Go?

Amafaranga yanjye yagiye he?

Open your own MoMo text messages and see, in seconds, who got your money, what went on airtime, bundles and power, and how much the fees cost you.

Your messages never leave this phone. No account, no upload, nothing saved. Close the page and it's gone.

How do I get all my MoMo messages at once?
  1. Android: install the free app SMS Backup & Restore from the Play Store.
  2. Tap Set up a backup, keep Messages on, turn Phone calls off, choose your phone as the place to save it, then back up.
  3. Come back here, tap Open my SMS backup and pick the file that starts with sms-. Only MoMo messages are read; everything else is skipped.
  4. iPhone or any phone: open the M-Money chat, press and hold a message, tap Copy, and paste it here. Repeat for as many as you like.
