Drop in a site energy log

A CSV of daily or monthly records per site. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): site_id, diesel_litres, run_hours, grid_kwh, optional date. Rows are summed per site.

Price and tariff are your inputs, set them to your contract values. Emission factors: diesel 2.68 kg CO₂ per litre (standard combustion factor); grid 0.461 kg CO₂ per kWh (Rwanda combined margin, IFI Dataset of Default Grid Factors v2.0, Jul 2019).
