Drop in zone volumes

A CSV of monthly volumes per zone or district metered area: water put in and water billed. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): zone (or DMA), month (YYYY-MM or a date), system_input_m3, billed_m3 (billed metered); optional billed_unmetered_m3, unbilled_authorised_m3, connections and mains_km. Method: IWA standard water balance. Non-revenue water = system input minus billed authorised consumption; water losses = non-revenue water minus unbilled authorised consumption.
