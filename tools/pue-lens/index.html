Drop in meter readings

A CSV of interval readings for the whole facility and the IT load, from your BMS, DCIM or energy meters. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): time, facility_kwh (all energy the site drew in the interval) and it_kwh (IT load energy, best metered at the UPS output or closer to the servers), or facility_kw and it_kw with the interval read from the timestamps; optional outdoor_temp_c and cooling_kwh. Method: PUE per ISO/IEC 30134-2 = total facility energy / IT equipment energy. CO₂ uses 0.461 kg per kWh, the grid factor used across this toolkit; enter yours to change it.
