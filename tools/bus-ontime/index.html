Drop in departures

A CSV of scheduled and actual departures per route, from your dispatch system or GPS. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): route, scheduled (date and time), actual (actual departure; empty = did not run); optional stop (terminal) and bus. Method: on time = from 1 min early to 5 min late; excess wait time = actual minus scheduled average wait for passengers arriving at random, per route and service day with 6 or more departures; bunched = a gap under half the scheduled gap.
