Drop in an ATM downtime log

A CSV of out-of-service episodes per ATM from your monitoring or switch, with start, end and reason. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): atm (terminal id), start and end of each out-of-service episode, reason or state (cash out, comms down, card reader fault, dispenser fault, power, maintenance, other), optional location or branch. Any time not in an episode counts as in service; the period is the whole calendar month(s) in the file; overlapping episodes on one ATM are merged, so no hour counts twice. An episode with no end runs to the end of the period.
