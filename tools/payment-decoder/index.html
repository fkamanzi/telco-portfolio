Drop in a transaction log

A CSV of card, ATM or transfer attempts with their response or reason codes. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): time, code (an ISO 8583 response code such as 00, 51 or 91, or an ISO 20022 status or reason such as ACSC, AB05 or AC01; a separate status column such as RJCT is also read), optional channel, counterparty (issuer, bank or wallet), amount, terminal and customer (a masked card or account reference, used to spot retries). Codes outside these two standards are ranked but not decoded.
