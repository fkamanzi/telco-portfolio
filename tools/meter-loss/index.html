Drop in meter reads

A CSV of monthly consumption per meter, from billing or prepaid vending, electricity or water. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): meter (or account), month (YYYY-MM or a date), consumption (kWh or m3); optional class (or tariff group), read_type (actual / estimated) and status (active / inactive). Method: each meter's baseline is the median of its months before the last 3, compared with the mean of the last 3; each meter is also compared with the median of its class.
