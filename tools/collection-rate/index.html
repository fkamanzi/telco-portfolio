Drop in billing records

A CSV of bills with the amount billed, the amount paid and their dates. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): account (the payer, shown masked), bill, period or billed_date, due_date, amount_billed, amount_paid, paid_date (last or full payment, blank if unpaid), optional group (school, class, zone, branch or property). Dates as YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY. Method: collection rate = paid / billed for bills due on or before the as-of date (blank: the latest billing or payment date in the file; payments dated after it are left out); ageing by days past due at that date. Amounts in RWF.
