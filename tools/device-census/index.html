Drop in your device list

A CSV with one row per connected device, or one row per model with a count. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): network (2G, 3G, 4G, LTE-M, NB-IoT), optional type (POS terminal, meter, tracker, alarm), site (branch or district), critical (yes or no), status (none, ordered, installed, tested), last_seen (date) and count. Method: devices that support 4G, LTE-M, NB-IoT or 5G are ready; 3G-at-best devices lose 3G on 30 June 2027; 2G-only devices stop when 2G is retired (working towards December 2028); a replacement counts as done once it is tested.
