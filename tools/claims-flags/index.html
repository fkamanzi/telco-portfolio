Drop in a claims export

A CSV of claims with the policy start, loss and report dates and the amount claimed. It is read in your browser and never leaves this page; insured and vehicle identifiers are masked on screen.

Expected columns (any order, matched loosely): claim, policy, insured, inception_date, loss_date, report_date, amount; optional paid, provider (garage, hospital or assessor), vehicle and class or cause. Dates as YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY. Red flags: a loss within your window of the start of cover; a report more than 30 days after the loss; the same insured or vehicle with an amount within 1% and losses within 90 days; an insured with 3 or more claims; a provider with 5 or more claims averaging over twice the median of the providers in its class. Amounts in exact multiples of RWF 100,000 are a weak flag and never count on their own.
